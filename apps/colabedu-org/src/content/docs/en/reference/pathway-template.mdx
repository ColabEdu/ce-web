The **`PathwayTemplate`** resource represents the **official curriculum framework** of a subject under an educational standard (e.g., *IB Spanish B SL*, *AP Spanish Language*, or *LOMLOE Spanish Literature*).

---

## Pedagogical Concept

Within the **Pedagogy as Code (OPS)** architecture, `PathwayTemplate` bridges the gap between statutory standards and classroom operations:

1. **Thematic Structuring:** Defines mandatory teaching units, core topics, and prescribed subtopics established by educational bodies (State Ministries, College Board, International Baccalaureate).
2. **Official Assessment Weighting:** Encapsulates instructional hour allocations and official examination weights (e.g., Paper 1: 25%, Paper 2: 50%, Individual Oral: 25%).
3. **Foundation for Course Instances:** Educators and schools do not construct curricula from scratch; they instantiate a [`CourseSpec`](/en/reference/course-spec/) that inherits from an official `PathwayTemplate` and specializes it into specific delivery modalities (Full Year, Intensive, or Remediation).
