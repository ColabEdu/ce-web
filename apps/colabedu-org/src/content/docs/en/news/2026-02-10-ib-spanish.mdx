Continuing with our Q1 2026 roadmap, we are proud to announce the integration of the **International Baccalaureate (IB) - Spanish B (Language B)** into ColabEdu's `Grading V2` assessment engine.

## Assessing under IB Criteria

The International Baccalaureate is characterized by a holistic assessment, structured through different Criteria (e.g., Criterion A: Language, Criterion B: Message, Criterion C: Conceptual Understanding). Especially in the Individual Oral exam, the workload for teachers during the *Internal Assessment* is enormous.

## Architectural Integration

To provide native IB support on our platform, we have extended our YAML specifications:

1. **C0 (IB Rubrics):** 
   Our global taxonomy (`taxonomy.loc.int-ib.yaml`) now encapsulates IB band descriptors. You no longer have to transcribe the guide document; the platform automatically selects it when configuring the course as "IB Diploma Programme".
   
2. **C1 and C2 (UI Interactivity):** 
   We have implemented new `Widgets` in the Flutter App to support visual stimuli (photographs) associated with the IB's transversal themes (Identities, Experiences, Human Ingenuity, Social Organization, Sharing the Planet).

3. **C3 (Deterministic Grading):** 
   The `SpecManager` restricts Artificial Intelligence models to evaluate argumentative essays and oral presentations exactly as an IBO examiner would, justifying the band score (e.g., 10-12 points) with direct references to the transcript.
