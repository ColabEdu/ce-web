And we're back with more updates for Spain! To complement our LOMLOE support, today we announce the launch of our support for the university access system in Spain: the **EBAU** (or EvAU, depending on the Autonomous Community).

## The 2nd Year of Bachillerato Bottleneck

The final year of Bachillerato is a long-distance race. Teachers of Spanish Language and Literature, History, and Foreign Language face enormous volumes of mock exams (text commentaries, 250-word essays, summaries). Manual grading time drastically limits the amount of *feedback* students receive before the actual exam.

## Standardized Regional Criteria in ColabEdu

The greatest difficulty of the EBAU is regional fragmentation: rubrics change by Autonomous Community.

1.  **Regional Taxonomies (C0):**
    Our directory hierarchy in `ce-specs` supports the *override* of properties at country and regional level (`loc.es`, `loc.es-madrid`, `loc.es-andalucia`). If a school is in Madrid, the system will automatically load the Grading Criteria of the Autonomous University of Madrid (for example, deducting exactly 0.25 points for a spelling mistake, according to the annual scale).

2.  **UI Interactivity (C1):**
    The "Learning Gym" (`app.colabedu.net`) allows students to input texts with strict time limits (locked timer), replicating the pressure of the actual exam.

3.  **Deterministic Inference (C3):**
    Through the `SpecManager` component, the AI acts as the most stringent university grader, generating detailed reports that separate the structural plane (coherence, cohesion) from the orthotypographical plane (spelling mistakes, accent marks).
