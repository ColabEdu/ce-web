## An Open and Collaborative Ecosystem

ColabEdu structures its educational technology around two distinct pillars: the **Open Assessment Standard (OAS)** as an open infrastructure for the community, and the **ColabEdu Agents Platform** as a commercial, deterministic, and sovereign suite for governments, institutions, and publishers:

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="For Educators & Students" icon="open-book">
    Daily support tools such as Teacher Copilots and interactive Socratic Tutors that free up bureaucratic burden and accompany students without giving away answers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="For Governments & Districts" icon="document">
    Federated governance to audit assessments at scale, ensuring legal compliance and providing tools to guard against "pedagogical drift."
  </Card>
  <Card title="For EdTech Providers" icon="puzzle">
    Mass modernization tools (*Modernizers*). Transform PDFs, textbooks, and legacy repositories into the new AI-powered assessment standard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="For Engineers & Developers" icon="setting">
    The OAS format (YAML) and the GitOps architecture are 100% open. We invite you to create your own implementations, integrate OAS into your LMS, or design new Agents.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
