:::caution[Restricted Resource - Partners Only]
Access and configuration of ColabEdu **Layer C3 resources (Directives, Personas, and Gems)** is restricted to authorized partner organizations (governments, partner publishers, and consultancies).
:::


The OPS Layer 3 (C3) `Directive` resource defines immutable behavioral rules or "hard" conditions for the AI evaluation engine.

Unlike rubrics (which define qualitative levels of competence), directives impose systematic quantitative penalties or global rules (e.g., deductions for spelling errors) that the LLM must inexorably execute during the correction process.
