**Architecture:** Open Assessment Standard (OAS v1beta1)  
**Layer:** C1 (Exercise Types / Structural Templates)

**Objective:** To define agnostic logical containers that multiply raw content (C2) and evaluate it against the legal rubrics (C0) of ANY subject.

In the ColabEdu architecture, an "Exercise Type" (C1 Layer) is an *empty skeleton, blind to the subject matter*. It contains no specific questions or laws. It contains the **User Interface (UI) logic** and the **base evaluation directive for the AI**.

When the *Automated Ingestion Pipeline* runs, it cross-references downloaded texts, maps, or formulas with these templates to generate millions of transversal combinations.

## 1. Domain: Written Production and Document Analysis
*Interfaces for processing information and generating argumentative responses. Useful for Language, History, Philosophy, and Biology.*

1. **Guided Split-Pane Essay (Standard Split-Pane):** 
   - *Use in Language:* Commentary on a poem.
   - *Use in History:* Read the Declaration of the Rights of Man (C2) and write an essay on its causes.
   - *Use in Science:* Read a "Paper" on biotechnology and summarize the hypothesis.
2. **Email Response / Specific Format:** The student assumes a role and responds.
3. **Restricted Objective Summary:** Severely limits length (e.g., max. 100 words). Penalizes if the student includes personal opinions or "copy-pastes."
4. **Analysis of Graphs and Infographics (Multimodal):** The C2 block contains an image/graph.
   - *Use in Geography:* Analyze a population pyramid from INEGI.
5. **Creative Writing / Hypothesis Continuation:** 
   - *Use in Philosophy:* Resolve an ethical dilemma by arguing from Kant's perspective.

## 2. Domain: Interactive Comprehension and Evaluation
*Exercises for deterministic auto-grading and guided analysis.*

6. **Advanced Multiple Choice Question (MCQ):** The AI generates *semantic distractors* based on the C2 text.
7. **Short Answer Questions:** The student answers in 1-2 sentences. The AI evaluates semantic precision.
8. **Fill in the Blanks (Cloze Text):** The system removes keywords from a C2 text.
9. **Logical Ordering (Drag & Drop):** Order chronological events or steps of the scientific method.
10. **Fake News Hunt (Fact-Checking):** A journalistic article (C2) is presented, and the student interacts with the bot to request data and verify the news.

## 3. Domain: Oral and Multimodal Interaction
*Uses Speech-to-Text (Whisper) models and image validation.*

11. **Interactive Socratic Tutor (Oral/Text Chatbot):** The student debates with the AI. (e.g., Historical Roleplay with Abraham Lincoln).
12. **Comparison Monologue / Oral Defense:** The student records an audio defending a thesis with a time limit.
13. **Image Description (Visual Stimulus):** The student records a description of an image (e.g., a plant cell).
14. **Listening Comprehension / Note-Taking:** Playback of a podcast or scientific lecture. The student answers questions.

## 4. Domain: Mechanics, Identification, and Tagging
*Exercises where the student marks, underlines, or associates elements within a canvas.*

15. **Inline Tagging (Syntax & Fact Tagger):** Underline elements in the text (e.g., causes in red, consequences in blue).
16. **Transformation / Indirect Editing:** A deficient text is presented, and the student must correct its errors (erroneous dates, grammatical flaws).
17. **Lexical Expansion / Key Concepts:** Define a technical term highlighted in a C2 text within the context of that subject.

## 5. The STEM "Delta": Future Required Molds
To cover hard sciences (Mathematics, Physics, Programming), the system can be expanded with these 4 molds, maintaining the same RAG engine and C0/C3:

18. **Equation Editor (Math/LaTeX Editor):** To solve calculus and physics step-by-step.
19. **Interactive Graphs (Cartesian Canvas):** To draw functions, parabolas, or vectors.
20. **Code Execution Environment (Code Sandbox):** Code editor with AI algorithmic evaluation.
21. **Interactive Map (GIS Point-and-Click):** Blank maps for Geography or Anatomy.

## The "Recipe Schema" (The Secret to Agnostic Evaluation)

If the UI is the same (e.g., Split-Pane Essay), how does the AI know it's grading a History exam and not a Language one? **The injection of the Rubric (C0)**.

- **Scenario A (Literature):** C1 (Essay) + C2 (Poem) + C0 (Rubric: Meter, Literary Figures).
- **Scenario B (History):** C1 (Essay) + C2 (1812 Constitution) + C0 (Rubric: Causes, Anachronisms). **The AI will ignore poetic meter.**

### Conclusion
The power of the **Lego Architecture (OAS V2)** lies in its absolute agnosticism. If ColabEdu decides to launch a product for Medical or Law Schools, **not a single new screen needs to be programmed**. All effort focuses on adding new texts (C2) and laws (C0).
