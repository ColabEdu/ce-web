Welcome to the official ColabEdu roadmap. Here we document our projected journey integrating the world's educational standards into our OAS v1beta1 architecture.

## Pilots and Beta Projects

- **[Pilot: SEP and MCCEMS Architecture for Mexico](/en/news/2026-04-20-sep-mexico/)** *(Projected: April 2026)*  
  National-level mapping of Mexican educational standards and CENEVAL / MEJOREDU Diagnostic Assessments.

- **[Proof of Concept: University Entrance Exam (EBAU/EvAU) Mock Tests](/en/news/2026-03-30-ebau/)** *(Projected: March 2026)*  
  Preparation for university access in Spain with automated evaluation of text commentaries and essays.

- **[Pilot: LOMLOE Integration (Spanish Educational System)](/en/news/2026-03-05-lomloe/)** *(Projected: March 2026)*  
  Adaptation to the basic knowledge, specific competencies, and evaluation criteria of the Spanish LOMLOE.

- **[Mapping in Progress: International Baccalaureate (IB Spanish B)](/en/news/2026-02-10-ib-spanish/)** *(Projected: February 2026)*  
  Support for the correction of oral and written interactions within the IB framework.

- **[Initial Mapping: AP Spanish Language and Culture](/en/news/2026-01-15-ap-spanish/)** *(Projected: January 2026)*  
  Integration testing of AP Spanish standards for the College Board ecosystem.
