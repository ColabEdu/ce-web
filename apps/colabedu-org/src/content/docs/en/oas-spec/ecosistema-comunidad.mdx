The mission to transform educational assessment through Artificial Intelligence cannot, and should not, be the isolated effort of a single company. At ColabEdu, we firmly believe in the power of collective intelligence, open collaboration, and **Digital Sovereignty**.

The **Open Assessment Specification (OAS)** standard is just the cornerstone. Our ultimate goal is to build a global ecosystem and a vibrant community where educators, engineers, publishers, and governments collaborate to improve education worldwide.

---

## 1. ColabEdu Open Projects

To ensure that the heart of education remains public and auditable, ColabEdu releases and maintains *Open Source* projects aimed at fostering standardization:

*   **The OAS Standard (v1beta1):** The entire YAML specification, JSON schemas, and architectural documentation are open for any entity to freely adopt.
*   **Base Libraries:** We encourage the creation of open repositories of *specs* (laws, public rubrics, and taxonomies) so that institutions and developers don't have to start from scratch.

The open model allows that if a university in Mexico improves a *Critical Thinking* rubric, a school district in Spain can analyze, adapt, and adopt it through a simple *Pull Request*.

## 2. OAS-Powered Solutions

While the OAS standard belongs to the community, at ColabEdu we develop **scalable and high-performance software solutions** based on this specification. These tools are designed to solve real-world problems at all levels of the educational system:

### 🎓 For Educators and Students
*   **Teacher Copilots:** Tools that assist teachers in creating exercises and rubrics, freeing them from bureaucratic burdens so they can focus on human interaction.
*   **Interactive Socratic Tutors:** Practice environments (Learning Gyms) for students that, unlike commercial LLMs (like ChatGPT), are "chained" to OAS directives to ensure the student is guided pedagogically and not given direct answers.

### 🏛️ For Governments and School Districts
*   **Pedagogical Drift Control:** Institutional dashboards that allow a district to verify that the artificial intelligence used in its classrooms is evaluating strictly under the laws and parameters of its region, without deviating towards ideological or corporate biases.
*   **Large-Scale Audit and Diagnosis:** Tools that analyze anonymized OAS assessment data to identify which specific competencies (e.g., *Reading Comprehension* or *Mathematical Reasoning*) require reinforcement at the district, state, or country level.

### 📚 For EdTech Providers and Publishers
*   **Modernization Tools (Modernizers):** AI pipelines designed to help traditional publishers ingest and transform their legacy editorial assets (textbooks and question banks in PDF) into the modern declarative OAS format.
*   **Integration APIs:** Interoperability systems so that existing LMS platforms (Moodle, Canvas) can connect to the ecosystem and benefit from *Deterministic Grading* without needing to rewrite their entire platform.
